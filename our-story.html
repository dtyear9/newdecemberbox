Who we are

Our Story

Christmas gifts should feel personal, joyful and genuinely lovely to open.

Made for the moments in between.

We believe the best Christmas memories are often the small ones: a hot drink after a cold walk, a film under a blanket, a box of treats shared around the table.

That is why each December Box is built around a feeling. We bring together familiar festive favourites and thoughtful extras to create gifts that invite people to slow down and enjoy December.

“A little box of Christmas, made to be opened slowly.”

We are building a warm, modern British gifting brand — premium without being stuffy, festive without being overwhelming.
