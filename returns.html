Returns

If your order arrives damaged or there is a problem with what you received, contact us as soon as possible with your order details and photographs of the issue.

Because hampers may contain food and seasonal items, return eligibility can depend on the condition and type of product. We will explain the available options when you contact us.

What to include

  • Your name and order details
  • A description of the issue
  • Photos if an item arrived damaged
  • The best way to contact you
